The Process

How It Works

01

Browse verified needs

Coaches, teachers, and administrators post specific funding needs. Each one is reviewed and verified before it goes live.

02

Give to what moves you

Choose the need that matters to you, give any amount once or monthly, and watch the bar move in real time.

03

Schools receive 88%

Funds are disbursed through Childs Play Foundation, our 501(c)(3) fiscal sponsor. You get an official tax receipt, every time.

From the Founder

Why I built AlumniUp

I am Risha Alexis, a graduate of Martin Luther King Jr. Senior High School. King High gave me the foundation to build a career in engineering and cybersecurity. But when I looked back, I saw coaches buying equipment out of their own pockets and programs struggling to survive.

AlumniUp is built for Detroit, by Detroit. Every dollar is accounted for, and every gift goes to the people shaping our kids: our coaches, teachers, and administrators.
