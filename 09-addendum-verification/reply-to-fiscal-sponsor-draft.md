Subject: How AlumniUp will verify schools used funds as stated

Hi [sponsor contact name],

Thank you for raising the verification question directly. You are right that our money trail, as first designed, proved a school received funds. It did not yet prove the school spent them on the stated need. We have now built that second check into the platform design, before any money moves. Here is how it works:

1. Agreement before money. Every school signs a participation agreement at registration. Funds are restricted to the stated need, proof of use is due within 30 days of receipt, unspent funds are returned or redirected only with your written approval, and a school with overdue proof cannot post new needs or receive new disbursements.

2. Proof of use is a required step, not a favor. After a school confirms receipt, the need enters an Awaiting Proof state. The poster uploads vendor invoices or purchase receipts and a completion photo showing the result, plus the final amount spent if it differs from the amount disbursed.

3. A named administrator countersigns. The school administrator reviews the packet and attests, by name and date, that the funds were spent as stated. The poster gathers the proof, the administrator signs for it, and your office verifies it. Two signatures, two different checks.

4. Your verification is the final public state. When your office marks a need Use Verified, that becomes the last step on its public trail: Live, Funded, Sent, Received, Use Verified. The public sees the summary (vendor, amount spent versus disbursed, completion photo, verified date). The underlying invoices stay on your side, since receipts can carry personal details.

5. Larger needs can carry structure. Above a threshold you set (we are using $5,000 as a working default), you may choose payment in tranches, with the balance released when proof for the first part is verified, or direct payment to the vendor against the invoice, so the school never holds the money.

6. The enforcement ladder is automatic. A reminder goes out at day 14. At day 30 the need shows Proof Overdue publicly and the school is flagged, with no new needs and no new disbursements, until the proof is filed. You can also export a complete evidence packet PDF for any need, covering the trail dates, the disbursement record, the receipt confirmation, the proof documents, and the attestation, for your audit and tax records.

The full mechanism is written into our stage documents as the Verification of Use Addendum, and I am glad to share that document if it is useful for your files. The one decision that is yours is the threshold for tranches and direct vendor payment. If $5,000 does not fit how you work, tell me the number you prefer and we will build to it.

Thank you for holding us to the stronger standard. It made the product better.
